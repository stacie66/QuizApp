# QUIZAPP 🚀

QUIZAPP is a powerful and intuitive learning tool designed to help students of all ages master their subjects through flashcards, summaries, and AI-generated questions.
 Whether you're a 5th grader or a university student, QUIZAPP makes learning efficient, engaging, and fun. 🌟

---

## Features ✨

### **Home Screen**
- Profile icon, menu icon, and search bar for easy navigation.
- Horizontal subject decks for quick access.
- **+ Button**: Upload files (PDFs, images, etc.) to generate flashcards and summaries.
- **Small Features**:
  - Share button for sharing decks or chapters.
  - Settings icon for app preferences (e.g., dark mode).
- Bottom navigation: Home, Flashcards, Decks, Profile.

---

### **Flashcard Screen**
- File upload progress and automatic redirection to the flashcard screen.
- **Summarization**: Display chapters from the PDF with generated flashcards and Q/A.
- **Flashcards**:
  - Automatically generated from summaries (bullets or key points).
  - Edit, delete, or shuffle flashcards.
- **Q/A**:
  - Multiple-choice questions generated by OpenAI.
  - Visual feedback: Correct answers (green) and incorrect answers (red).
  - Display a score at the end of each chapter.
- **Image Upload**: Upload images (e.g., external papers) and generate questions from them.

---

### **Decks Feature**
- Organized structure: Subjects → Chapters → Flashcards/Q&A.
- Centralized management for all subjects and chapters.
- Syncs with the Flashcards screen for seamless access.

---

### **Profile Screen**
- Display user name, streaks (days visited), flashcards viewed, and flashcards memorized.
- **Progress Tracking**: Track progress for each subject/chapter (e.g., % completed).
- **Achievements**: Earn badges for milestones like "100 Flashcards Memorized."

---

### **Sharing**
- Share chapters’ flashcards and Q/A with others.
- Optionally, share individual flashcards.

---

## How It Works 🛠️
1. **Upload a File**: Upload a PDF or image to generate summaries, flashcards, and questions.
2. **Learn with Flashcards**: Study automatically generated flashcards and edit them as needed.
3. **Test Your Knowledge**: Answer AI-generated multiple-choice questions and track your progress.
4. **Organize Your Decks**: Manage your subjects, chapters, and flashcards in one place.
5. **Track Your Progress**: Monitor your learning journey with streaks, achievements, and progress tracking.

---

## Tech Stack 💻
- **Frontend**: Flutter (Dart)
- **Backend**: Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage)
- **AI Integration**: OpenAI API (for summarization and Q/A generation)

   ```bash
   flutter run
